About

Where digital logic meets living systems

A place to learn in the open — pairing binary logic with biological systems to build things that might genuinely help people, and to share how they work.

  • Human-centered AI systems Intelligent solutions that enhance human capability and quality of life through adaptive, responsive technology.
  • Smart agriculture & sustainable environments AI-powered systems that optimize resource usage and promote sustainable practices across agricultural and urban environments.
  • Assistive technologies for health & mobility Next-generation assistive devices that restore independence and enhance accessibility for people with diverse needs.

What's being explored

A few of the ideas in progress across health, agriculture, and the edge. Some are built and open-source; most are still concepts.

01

NeuraLink Bridge X1

A next-generation brain-computer interface enabling direct neural control of digital devices, with ultra-low-latency signal processing.

 02

DoseXTrack

An AI-powered platform for tracking and optimizing medication dosage — keeping patients safe and treatment effective.

 03

HydroMind Controller

An intelligent hydroponics system that uses AI to monitor plant health and adjust nutrients, pH, and lighting automatically.
