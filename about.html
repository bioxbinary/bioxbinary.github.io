Mission

Why this exists

Bioxbinary is an independent lab I build in public — a place to pair the logic of binary with the complexity of biology, and to share what I learn along the way. It exists because this work feels worth doing, and worth giving away.

  • Assistive technologies for human enhancement Exploring neural interfaces and intelligent mobility — with the aim of restoring autonomy and amplifying human potential.
  • Sustainable systems for the planet Exploring AI-driven agricultural and hydroponic systems that use resources wisely, in support of food security and ecological balance.
  • Bioethics-coded intelligence Holding every line of code and every biological idea to an ethical standard that safeguards human dignity and a shared environment.
