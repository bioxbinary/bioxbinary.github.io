Origin & Mission

Why this lab exists

Bioxbinary is an independent deep-tech lab. It was created to pair the crisp, deterministic logic of binary computing with the delicate, adaptive realities of biological care.

Most modern healthcare technology suffers from predatory cloud subscription models, vendor lock-in, and fragile dependencies on external servers. When an elderly person or a child with special needs relies on an assistive device, that device must work 100% of the time—even if the internet goes down.

We believe the most humane technology is local-first, open-source, and physically repairable. We build tangible devices on real microcontrollers, share all schematics and firmware openly, and operate with an AI-augmented workflow that allows a tiny lab to innovate with the speed of an entire engineering organization.

  • 01 · Assistive Technologies for Human Autonomy Building non-stigmatizing, accessible hardware—from smart medication adherence dispensers like DoseXTrack to sensory augmentation aids—engineered to restore self-reliance and peace of mind.
  • 02 · Local-First Data Sovereignty Zero patient data extraction. All telemetry, dosing logs, and biometric inferences run locally on deterministic microcontrollers over MQTT and local networks without leaking private health data to corporate clouds.
  • 03 · Open Hardware & Transparent Engineering Every PCB schematic, 3D CAD model, and line of FreeRTOS firmware is published under permissive open-source licenses. Care should never be locked behind a paywall.
  • 04 · Small Lab, AI-Assisted Workflow A deliberately small, hands-on lab. AI tools speed up firmware drafting, documentation, and research notes, while every device is still built, bench-tested, and reviewed by hand.
The Lab Workbench

How we build

The physical and computational tools powering Bioxbinary experiments.

HW

Embedded Microcontrollers

ESP32-S3, ESP32-WROOM, ARM Cortex-M0/M4, FreeRTOS, ESP-IDF, I2C/SPI sensor buses, hardware RTC timers.

NET

Local Messaging & Protocols

Mosquitto MQTT brokers, QoS 1 determinism, WebSockets, BLE 5.0, TLS 1.3 ephemeral cryptography.

FAB

Rapid Physical Prototyping

Custom parametric 3D CAD design, PETG/PLA additive manufacturing, ergonomic tactile switches, breadboard testbenches.
